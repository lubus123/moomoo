Key insight: Cheese type selection affects cream available to butter plants.
Mozzarella (higher fat cheese) sends less excess cream than Parmesan (lower fat).

Cream Flow Dependencies

Separator → splits whole milk into cream + skim based on target cream fat %
Cheese Plants → receive skim, standardize for target cheese type → excess cream sent to cream pool
Cream Pool → aggregates separator cream + cheese plant excess cream
Butter Plants → draw from cream pool, churn into butter + buttermilk → dry into powder
